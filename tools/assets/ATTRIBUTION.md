# Assets of the Poligon release archive: authors, sources and licenses

The archive `cooked/assets.pak` holds the assets below, cooked by Poligon's `asset_cooker`
(textures block-compressed and some made smaller, meshes with simpler levels of detail made
in Blender, animations converted to ozz-animation archives). Licenses were checked at their
sources on 2026-10-26.

- **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): no conditions; the authors
  are named here as thanks.
- **CC-BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): may be shared and changed for
  any purpose, as long as the authors are credited as below. Changes made: converted to
  Poligon's cooked format.
- **MIT** (text below): may be used for any purpose, as long as the copyright notice and the
  license text go with it.

## Made by Poligon itself (MIT, the license of Poligon)

- The land: made by the engine's terrain generator from the settings in `forest.level`.
- Tree meshes (pine, spruce, birch, oak, two of each, young pine and spruce): generated by
  `tools/assets/make_tree.mjs` with EZ-Tree (https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree, MIT,
  Copyright (c) 2024 Daniel Greenheck) from our recipes; the textures on them are listed below.
- Tree impostors (pictures of the trees from many sides): baked by Poligon's `impostor_baker`.
- Reeds, cattails, water lilies, grass blades and flowers: made by code at run time, not stored.
